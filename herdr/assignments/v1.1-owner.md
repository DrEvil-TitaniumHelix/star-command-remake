## 5. Map zoom with the mouse
Battlefield (PLBATTLE 7x7) and the other map screens (SPACE galaxy/sector, INSIDE location map): mouse wheel zooms in/out around the pointer; LMB click = zoom in one step on that spot, RMB click = zoom out (no context menu). Where LMB already means "click this hotkey/cell" (v1.1), the hotkey click wins and zoom-in uses wheel/double-click: document the choice per screen.

## 6. Tooltips on inventory items
Hovering (and gamepad: focused item) any listed item — weapons, armor, equipment, ship parts, hull/ship offers, crew candidates — shows a tooltip with its stats and cost: for weapons damage / hits / ammo / reloads / range / weight / price, for armor absorption / weight / price, for ships hull / cargo / fuel / weapons slots / price, for crew the class/skills/wage.
